# Late-delivery risk: results

Generated by `python src/report.py` from `reports/*.json`. Every run is also in MLflow (`mlflow ui --backend-store-uri sqlite:///mlflow.db`).

## 1. Experiments (validation = Jan-Mar 2018)

| Model | ROC AUC | PR AUC | Late orders caught in top 10% | Precision in top 10% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| rule_seller_late_rate | 0.563 | 0.172 | 12.4% | 18.1% |
| logreg | 0.705 | 0.302 | 26.0% | 37.9% |
| hist_gb | 0.649 | 0.244 | 21.2% | 31.0% |
| xgboost | 0.661 | 0.254 | 22.3% | 32.5% |

**Chosen:** `logreg` (best validation PR AUC), refit on train+valid, scored once on the untouched Apr-Aug 2018 test months.

| Test (Apr-Aug 2018) | ROC AUC | Late orders caught in top 10% | Lift |
|---|---|---|---|
| logreg | 0.702 | 23.2% | 2.32x |
| seller-history rule | 0.521 | 12.5% | 1.25x |

Why the linear model won: the late rate swings from 1.4% to 21% month to month (Black Friday 2017, Feb-Mar 2018). The tree models fit 2017's patterns closely and generalized worse to 2018; the regularized linear model ranked more stably.

## 2. Operational findings (SQL, `src/insights.py`)

- Late orders average **2.57** stars vs **4.29** on time; 54.0% of late orders get a 1-2 star review vs 9.2%.
- Cross-state orders run late 9.3% of the time vs 6.1% within a state.
- Worst destination state with 500+ orders: MA at 19.7% late.
